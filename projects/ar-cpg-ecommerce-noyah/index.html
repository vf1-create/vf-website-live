Noyah Cosmetics immersive AR store

Step right into Noyah Cosmetics’ digital flagship. We reimagined online beauty shopping as an immersive 3D store where customers can browse, compare, and shop as if they’re in a brick-and-mortar boutique – only better. Products, textures, and tones come alive in the AR world just for you.

Ready to turn your e-commerce into a new experience?

About this project

Shopping for clean beauty shouldn’t feel like scrolling a catalog – it should feel like stepping into a world. Noyah Cosmetics came to Virtual Fudge with a bold idea: turn their online store into an experience that feels as tactile and immersive as their products.

  • The challenge

    Noyah Cosmetics, a clean beauty brand with a cult following, wanted to elevate its online shopping experience beyond flat photos and static PDPs. Their big question: what if an online store could feel as tangible as walking into a boutique?

  • The solution

    Virtual Fudge built a 3D virtual beauty store application using the Unity engine, designed to bring Noyah Cosmetics’ product universe to life. Shoppers can explore the entire range in an immersive digital space – zooming into packages, spinning product displays, and tapping to reveal details, ingredients, and stories. A built-in AR try-on feature lets users test shades in real time, seamlessly integrated into the flow of exploration.

  • The results

    The AR-powered environment recreates the joy of in-person discovery while keeping a direct path to purchase. Instead of skimming static pages, customers browse, play, and compare, gaining confidence with every interaction. The virtual store looks impressive, and it brings real cart conversions – helping Noyah Cosmetics build trust, increase engagement, and convert more visitors into buyers.

Key features

  • 3D virtual storefront: Navigate a fully interactive environment where shoppers explore products as if in person – spin, zoom, and tap to learn more.
  • Integrated AR try-on: Instant, browser-based shade testing enhances the decision process without leaving the 3D experience.
  • Seamless checkout flow: Add to cart and purchase directly inside the environment.

FAQ

We know AR can sound techy, so here are answers to the most common questions about AR for CPG e-commerce.

  • Do shoppers need to download an app?

    Not always. For Noyah Cosmetics, we created a dedicated beauty app with an AR try-on feature because it fits their long-term product and loyalty strategy – however, that’s just one approach. For many brands we use WebAR, which runs directly in the mobile browser from a QR code, product page, or link in bio – with no downloads or extra friction. Whether we recommend an app or a browser-based experience depends entirely on your customers, goals, budget, and how often you want shoppers to return.

  • How accurate is the AR try-on?

    Very. We combine ARKit face tracking, real-time lighting adaptation, and AI color calibration to mirror real-world shades with uncanny precision. Whether it’s golden-hour glow or clinical bathroom lighting, customers see what they’ll actually get, not an idealized Instagram filter. Confidence sells, and accuracy seals the deal.

  • How is ROI measured for AR try-ons?

    Every interaction tells a story. We track funnel events like AR opens, dwell time, virtual shade swaps, add-to-cart actions, and conversion rates – then compare them against non-AR sessions. As a result, Noyah Cosmetics sees clear data showing how AR boosts engagement, lowers returns, and lifts conversions. Less guesswork, more growth.

  • Will AR slow down my site?

    Not a chance. Our experiences are lightweight, mobile-optimized, and hosted on fast edge networks, so load times stay zippy even when the content’s 3D or hyper-realistic. Think of it as a digital magic trick – big impact, tiny footprint.
