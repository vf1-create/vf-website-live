A cube of fudge, lit in warm brass light, its top slice lifted slightly.

II. Virtual Try-On

Foot traffic is priceless. Point a phone at the window: try the lipstick on your own face, drop the sofa into your living room, and buy it knowing it fits.

Try-ons and 3D products that answer “will it fit?” before the fitting room does (and long before the returns desk).

Press and hold.

The cube unfolds into store shelving; one product lifts forward and turns.

IIII. Interactive Packaging

Shoppers want to try before they buy. Scan the box, and it comes alive: a story blooming out of the lid, the ingredients in 3D, and checkout one tap away.

Packaging that talks back: one scan opens the brand’s story, the ingredients and the checkout.

Press and hold.

The cube becomes a carton; its lid opens and a small scene builds itself above it.

IIIIII. Medical Education

Healthcare doesn’t need hype; it needs clarity you can trust. AR steps laid over the device, a simulated patient to practice on, and details we never fudge.

Clinical simulation, device training and bedside AR guides, built with your clinicians.

Press and hold.

The cube becomes a stylized beating heart; a scanning plane shows a cross-section.

IVIV. Fan Engagement

Stadiums are loud. Your brand should be louder. Live stats floating over the field, a 3D view from your seat before you buy, and an AR hunt that ends in high fives.

Seat-view previews, second-screen stats, citywide AR hunts and sponsor mini-games, all tracked to tickets and merch. Game day, every day.

Press and hold.

The cube becomes a stadium bowl of tiny fans; a wave travels around it.

VV. Brand Activations

Marketing has never been noisier, or harder to break through. So don’t shout. Let them play: an AR mirror that previews the result, a business card with a talking hologram.

Booth-stoppers like our Magic Mirror and the Living Hologram: people play and share, and every scan can become a lead.

Press and hold.

The cube becomes a booth floor; drifting figures gather toward it and form a line.

VIVI. Living Exhibits

The “do not touch” era is over. Point a phone at the marble and the paint comes back. Point it at the bones and the dinosaur walks again.

AR guides, multilingual docents and living exhibits that bring the archive out of storage, and tell you which stories keep visitors longest.

Press and hold.

The cube becomes a classical torso with missing pieces, which restore in brass and then in color.

VIIVII. Workforce Training

Let’s be honest: manuals are outdated. (Nobody read them anyway.) So we put AR steps on the machine, right where the hands are, until guessing turns into knowing.

Step-by-step AR instructions right on the equipment for onboarding, assembly and maintenance, with every completed step logged for the audit.

Press and hold.

The cube becomes an exploded product assembly that puts itself together one part at a time.
